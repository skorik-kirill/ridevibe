Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

In short. RideVibe collects only what it needs to work: your email to sign in, the rides and garage records you create, and your location while you ride. We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics services. You can delete your account and all data right in the app.

Who is responsible for your data

RideVibe is developed and operated by Kyrylo Skoryk, an individual based in Ukraine. For any question about your data, email ridevibe.app@gmail.com.

With or without an account

You can use RideVibe without an account: everything then stays on your phone only (apart from the map and weather requests described below).

If you sign in, your data is synced to our server so it is not lost when you change phones, and group rides become available.

Location

  • RideVibe accesses your location only while you record a ride or have a group ride's live map on. During that time recording continues in the background, and iOS shows an indicator.
  • Outside of a ride the app does not track your location.
  • On the live map your location is visible only to the participants of that ride. When you leave the live map, your current position is deleted from the server.
  • You can change location access at any time in iPhone Settings → RideVibe.

Who else sees or processes data

  • Other participants of your group ride see your name, role and, while the live map is on, your location and speed. Nobody but you sees your rides, garage or SOS contacts.
  • Supabase — hosting for our database and files (servers in the EU, Ireland). Stores account data on our behalf.
  • OpenFreeMap — provides map tiles. A request contains the map area you are viewing and your IP address, but not your account.
  • Open-Meteo — weather service. At the start of a ride we send it coordinates to get the air temperature, without any data about you.
  • Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze — if you tap “Route”, the destination opens in the app you choose, and its own policy applies.

We do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not use it for advertising or for tracking you across apps.

How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept until you delete it.
  • You can delete any single item (a ride, service record or group ride) at any time — it is removed from the server too.
  • Your live-map position is deleted as soon as you leave the live map.
  • Technical server logs (IP address, request time) are kept briefly, only for security and debugging.

How to delete your account and data

In the app: Motorcycle → profile icon → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, rides with tracks, garage and receipt photos, SOS contacts, the group rides you created and your participation in others — from the server and from the phone. If you no longer have the app, email ridevibe.app@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete everything manually within 30 days.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correct or delete it, restrict processing or withdraw consent (for example, by turning off location access). Just email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights, or the authority in your EU country of residence.

Security

All data between the app and the server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each user can see only their own data and the data of rides they participate in.

Children

RideVibe is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date, and we will notify you in the app about significant changes.
